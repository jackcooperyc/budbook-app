How It Works

Scan. Log. Learn. Optimize.

01 · Scan Product
Point camera at any dispensary product's QR code. COA data auto-fills: THC%, CBD%, terpene ratios, batch ID.
02 · Log Session
Record method, dose, time, mood, pain level (1-10), anxiety, and any wellness notes post-session.
03 · Buddy AI Learns
AI correlates your terpene and cannabinoid data against your logged outcomes. Identifies your personal "wellness fingerprint."
04 · Get Personalized Recs
Buddy surfaces new products, terpene combos, and dosing schedules proven to work for your body — not generic averages.
